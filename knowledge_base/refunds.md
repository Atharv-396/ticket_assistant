# Refunds Policy

1. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
2. A refund may be approved when:
   - A valid return is approved under the Returns Policy.
   - Damage is confirmed under the Damaged Goods Policy.
   - A defect is confirmed under the Defective Products Policy.
   - A wrong item was delivered and a replacement is unavailable.
   - An order is cancelled before dispatch under the Cancellation Policy.
3. Refunds are not issued for:
   - Change-of-mind returns on food products.
   - Change-of-mind returns on opened non-food products.
   - Claims reported outside the applicable time windows.
4. If the reason for a refund request is unclear, request more information before approving or rejecting.
5. Partial refunds may be issued when only part of an order is affected.
