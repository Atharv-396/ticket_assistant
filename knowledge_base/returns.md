# Returns Policy

1. Unopened non-food products may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery for a full refund (change of mind).
2. Opened non-food products are not eligible for a change-of-mind return.
3. Food products are not eligible for change-of-mind returns after delivery, even if unopened.
4. Damaged or defective products are handled under the Damaged Goods Policy or Defective Products Policy rather than this policy.
5. If product type, opened/unopened status, or delivery date is missing and is necessary to decide eligibility, request more information.
6. Returns outside the 14-day window are not eligible unless the product is damaged or defective.
